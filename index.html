About Me

Hey! I'm Mohammed, and yeah, I'm a web developer. But let's be real—I'm that guy who spends way too much time tinkering with code, breaking stuff just to see if I can fix it (usually with coffee in hand). My coding journey started back in high school when I accidentally deleted my first website and had to rebuild it from scratch. Lesson learned: backups are important, but so is curiosity!

What drives me? Making the web a little more fun and accessible for everyone. I've got a soft spot for pixel art (as you can see from my rocket obsession) and retro games—my all-time favorite is Pac-Man, even though I still get eaten by ghosts half the time. When I'm not debugging or learning the latest JavaScript framework, you'll find me doodling UI ideas on napkins or debating whether tabs or spaces are superior (I use both, don't judge).

Oh, and I'm based in the UAE, which means my code has a bit of that desert heat in it—passionate, sometimes unpredictable, but always aiming for something awesome. Let's chat about your project—I promise I won't bore you with tech jargon unless you ask!
